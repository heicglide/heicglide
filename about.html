About

Simple conversion, built with privacy in mind.

HEIC Glide helps people create compatible JPG copies without unnecessary accounts, uploads, or complexity.

Our purpose: Make HEIC compatibility problems easier to solve without requiring an account or uploading photos to our server for conversion.

What HEIC Glide does

HEIC Glide is a focused browser tool for creating JPG copies of HEIC and HEIF photos. It is designed for common tasks such as email attachments, application forms, document uploads, and sharing with devices that do not support HEIC.

Privacy by design

Conversion is designed to happen locally in your browser. We do not intentionally collect the images you select. Optional analytics loads only after consent and does not receive photos or filenames.

How we approach guidance

Our guides aim to be practical, honest, and easy to verify. We do not claim that lossy conversion is perfectly lossless, and we recommend keeping original photos as master copies.

Independent project

HEIC Glide is an independent utility and is not affiliated with Apple, Microsoft, or Google. Product names belong to their respective owners.
