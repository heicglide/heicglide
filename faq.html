HEIC to JPG questions, answered.

Everything you need to know about converting HEIC and HEIF images with HEIC Glide.

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

No. The conversion is designed to happen inside your browser. Your chosen files are not uploaded to HEIC Glide for conversion.

Does HEIC Glide work on iPhone?

Yes. Open the converter in Safari, choose photos from your device, and download the JPG result. A modern browser and JavaScript are required.

Can I convert multiple photos?

Yes. Select or drop multiple .heic or .heif files. Each converted JPG appears with its own download button.

What is the difference between HEIC and JPG?

HEIC is a newer, efficient image format commonly used by Apple devices. JPG is older and more broadly supported by websites, apps, forms, and Windows computers.

Is HEIC Glide free?

Yes. The converter is free to use and does not require an account or signup.

Why did a file fail to convert?

Try a modern version of Safari, Chrome, Edge, or Firefox. Very large or damaged files may also fail. Refreshing the page and trying the original photo again can help.
