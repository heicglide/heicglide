Are my photos uploaded to a server?

No. HEIC Glide is designed to process your selected files inside your browser. Your photos are not sent to our server for conversion.

Can I convert more than one photo?

Yes. Select multiple HEIC or HEIF files at once, or drop them into the converter together. Each JPG can be downloaded when it is ready.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes. Open HEIC Glide in Safari, choose your photos, and download the converted JPG files. A modern browser with JavaScript is required.
