Common questions

What is a HEIC file?

HEIC is an efficient photo format commonly used by iPhone and Apple devices. It saves storage space but is not accepted everywhere.

Can I convert HEIC to JPG on iPhone?

Yes. Open HEIC Glide in Safari, choose your photos, and download the converted JPG files.

Is HEIC Glide free?

The basic converter is free to use. No account is required to get started.
