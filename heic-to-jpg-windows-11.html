How to convert HEIC to JPG on Windows 11

Make iPhone photos compatible with Windows apps, online forms, email, and photo editors—without installing complicated software.

Quick answer: Open the HEIC Glide converter, choose your HEIC photos, and download the JPG results. Conversion is designed to happen locally in your browser.

Why Windows users convert HEIC to JPG

Apple devices often save photos as HEIC because the format can preserve good image quality while using less storage. However, some Windows programs, business websites, upload forms, and older photo tools may not accept it. JPG remains one of the most widely supported image formats.

Converting a copy to JPG is useful when you need to share an iPhone photo with someone, upload it to a website, add it to a document, or open it in an application that does not support HEIC.

Convert HEIC to JPG in three steps

1. Open HEIC Glide on Windows 11

Use a current version of Microsoft Edge, Google Chrome, or Firefox. Open the HEIC to JPG converter. No account or signup is required.

2. Choose your HEIC photos

Select Choose HEIC files, then pick one or more files ending in .heic or .heif. You can also drag supported photos into the converter area on a desktop computer.

3. Download the JPG results

Wait while your browser converts the selected image. When the status says the JPG is ready, select Download. Your original HEIC photo stays unchanged, and the JPG is saved as a separate file.

Is browser-based HEIC conversion private?

HEIC Glide is designed to process your selected files locally in the browser instead of uploading them to our server for conversion. This helps reduce unnecessary exposure of personal photos. For sensitive files, you should still use a trusted device and keep your browser updated.

What if Windows still cannot open the JPG?

  • Confirm the downloaded filename ends in .jpg.
  • Try opening it with the Windows Photos app or a modern browser.
  • Repeat the conversion using the original HEIC file if the source photo may be damaged.
  • Update your browser if the conversion does not finish.

HEIC vs. JPG on Windows 11

HEIC is efficient and works well inside the Apple ecosystem. JPG usually offers broader compatibility with websites, office tools, Windows software, and social platforms. Keep the original HEIC file when you want to preserve your source photo, and use a JPG copy when compatibility matters most.

Frequently asked questions

Can Windows 11 open HEIC files?

Some Windows 11 computers can open HEIC files after the required image extensions are installed. Even then, a specific website or application may still require JPG.

Can I convert multiple HEIC photos?

Yes. HEIC Glide supports selecting multiple HEIC or HEIF files. Each successful conversion receives its own download button.

Will converting delete my original photo?

No. Conversion creates a new JPG download. It does not remove or replace the original HEIC file on your computer.

Is HEIC Glide free?

Yes. The converter is free to use and does not require an account.
