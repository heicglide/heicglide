iPhone formats

Why are iPhone photos HEIC?

Understand the storage benefits, compatibility tradeoffs, and the Most Compatible camera setting.

Short answer: iPhones use HEIC because it can store high-quality photos more efficiently than traditional JPG files.

HEIC saves storage space

Apple supports HEIF photos because the format offers better compression than JPEG. That can reduce storage use on a device and in a photo library while maintaining similar visual quality.

Why compatibility problems still happen

HEIC support is common on modern Apple devices, but some older computers, websites, business systems, and upload forms still expect JPG. That is why a photo can look normal on an iPhone but fail somewhere else.

How to make future iPhone photos use JPG

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Camera.
  3. Tap Formats.
  4. Select Most Compatible.

This changes future captures. It does not convert photos already stored as HEIC.

Apple explains HEIF, compatibility, and the Most Compatible setting in Using HEIF or HEVC media on Apple devices.

Frequently asked questions

Does Most Compatible convert old photos?

No. It affects new photos and videos only.

Is HEIC a bad format?

No. It is efficient and modern. JPG is simply more universally compatible.
