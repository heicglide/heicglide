How to convert HEIC to JPG on iPhone

Create an easy-to-share JPG copy of an iPhone photo using Safari—without installing another app or creating an account.

Quick answer: Open HEIC Glide in Safari, choose your HEIC photos, wait for local conversion, and download the JPG results.

Why iPhone photos use the HEIC format

iPhones commonly save photos as HEIC because the format can keep good image quality while using less storage. That is helpful on your phone, but some upload forms, older apps, business systems, and non-Apple devices may still ask for JPG files.

You do not need to replace your original photo. Instead, create a JPG copy when compatibility matters and keep the HEIC original in your Photos library.

Convert HEIC to JPG on iPhone in three steps

1. Open HEIC Glide in Safari

Open the HEIC to JPG converter in Safari. Make sure JavaScript is enabled and use a current version of iOS for the best experience.

2. Choose your HEIC photos

Tap Choose HEIC files. Select one or more photos from your iPhone. If prompted, allow Safari to access the photos you choose.

3. Download the converted JPG

Wait for the conversion to finish, then tap Download. Depending on your Safari settings, the JPG may appear in Downloads or the Files app. You can open, save, or share it from there.

Convert an iPhone photo now

Does the photo leave your iPhone?

HEIC Glide is designed to process selected photos locally inside your browser rather than uploading them to our server for conversion. Your original photo stays in your library, and the JPG is created as a separate download.

When is a JPG copy useful?

  • Uploading an image to a form that does not accept HEIC
  • Sending a photo to a Windows user or older application
  • Adding a photo to a document or presentation
  • Sharing with a website that specifically requests JPG

How to make future iPhone photos use JPG

If you prefer broader compatibility for new photos, open Settings → Camera → Formats and select Most Compatible. This affects future photos; it does not convert HEIC images already stored on your phone. Settings labels can vary slightly by iOS version.

Frequently asked questions

Can I convert HEIC to JPG without installing an app?

Yes. HEIC Glide runs in Safari and does not require an app installation or account.

Can I convert multiple iPhone photos?

Yes. You can choose multiple HEIC or HEIF photos. Each successful conversion receives its own download button.

Will conversion delete my original photo?

No. The original remains in your Photos library. The JPG is a separate file.

Why can’t a website accept my iPhone photo?

The website may not support HEIC uploads. A JPG copy is widely compatible and usually resolves the issue.
